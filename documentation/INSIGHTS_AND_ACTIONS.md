# Findings & Recommended Actions

## 1. Delinquency is broader than observed default

**Evidence:**  
10,069 customers show delinquency across the observed repayment-status history, compared with 6,636 observed defaults.

**Implication:**  
Delinquency provides a broader monitoring signal than the final default label alone.

**Recommended action:**  
Use repeated delinquency alongside exposure and payment behavior when defining analytical monitoring populations.

---

## 2. Payment stress is identifiable

**Evidence:**  
3,495 customers have a positive bill but no recorded payment. This represents 12.75% of customers with positive bills.

**Implication:**  
The positive-bill/no-payment segment provides a distinct repayment-behavior signal for further review.

**Recommended action:**  
Track this segment together with delinquency and exposure indicators rather than using default status as the only monitoring signal.

---

## 3. Credit exposure adds important context

**Evidence:**  
The portfolio contains approximately NT$5.02B in total credit limit across 30,000 customers.

**Implication:**  
Exposure concentration provides important context when interpreting repayment and delinquency patterns.

**Recommended action:**  
Review exposure bands together with repayment and delinquency measures when investigating portfolio segments.

---

## 4. Portfolio Payment Coverage is a useful monitoring KPI

**Evidence:**  
Six-period portfolio Payment Coverage is 11.72% under the project's documented definition of total recorded payments divided by total positive bills.

**Implication:**  
Payment Coverage provides a portfolio-level view of repayment activity that complements default and delinquency measures.

**Recommended action:**  
Track Payment Coverage alongside delinquency, payment behavior and exposure metrics during portfolio monitoring.

---

## Important limitation

These findings are descriptive observations from a historical public credit-card dataset. They do not establish causality, guarantee future default behavior, or represent automated lending decisions.

All metrics are based on the project's documented definitions and cross-tool validation controls.
